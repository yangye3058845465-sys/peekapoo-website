Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the PeekaPoo app (HarmonyOS, Android, iPhone and web) collects, why, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

Who we are

PeekaPoo is a student project by Team InsideOut, Universiti Sains Malaysia, built for the Huawei ICT Competition 2026–2027. Questions about this policy: CONTACT_EMAIL.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash; we cannot read them.
  • Health results: Digestive Score, Bristol stool type, Hydration Score, urine colour level and gas sensor readings, with the time of each toilet visit. In the current demo, new accounts receive simulated results.
  • Questions you ask the AI assistant, which are used only to generate the answer and are not stored.

We do not collect photos or video from your phone, your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. On a PeekaPoo device, camera images are analysed on the device and never uploaded; only scores and trends leave the device.

How we use it

  • To show your scores, trends and alerts and to compare readings with your own personal baseline.
  • To let the AI assistant explain your own results in plain language.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

Where it is stored

Your account and results are stored on our server hosted by PythonAnywhere (United States). All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS. Each account can see only its own data.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete your account. In the app, go to Profile › Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all of your results. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

PeekaPoo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not a medical device

PeekaPoo is a wellness screening aid, not a medical diagnosis. If you feel unwell, or notice blood, black stool or severe pain, see a doctor.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
