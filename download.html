iPhone

Home screen app

Add PeekaPoo to your home screen from Safari. It opens full screen, just like an app, with nothing to install.

  1. Tap Open PeekaPoo below in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open PeekaPoo from your home screen and sign up.

Good to know

It's a demo

New accounts start with two weeks of sample readings, so you can explore every screen before a PeekaPoo device is installed.

Only you see your data

Each account sees its own results and nothing else. Images never leave the device. Only scores and trends sync.

Not a diagnosis

PeekaPoo is a wellness screening aid. If you feel unwell or notice blood, black stool or severe pain, see a doctor.
